Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?
Progress, coins, boosters, unlocked items and settings are stored on your device. Online features also store completed-level records for your Softies account and leaderboard score. These records are not a full backup of all local items and settings. Removing the app does not delete your online account.

Do I need an account?
You do not need an email address or password to play. Online features create or use a Unity player account. On supported Apple devices, Game Center can link or recover that account. Offline play is available.

How do I delete my account and game data?
You can request account and data deletion by email at any time, including after uninstalling the game. You do not need to reinstall it to submit a request. If your version includes it, you can also open Settings › Privacy › Delete account and game data and confirm. Stay connected to the internet and keep the game open until deletion finishes. In-game deletion removes your Softies account, cloud progress and leaderboard entry and resets progress, coins and unlocked items on this device. Your Apple and Game Center accounts are not deleted. You can then continue offline. The deletion instructions also explain requests involving analytics, advertising or support data.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?
Use Settings › Privacy › Report an ad, or email an ad report. Include the advertised app or product, roughly when it appeared, your device and game version, and a screenshot if available. Please avoid including unrelated personal information. You choose whether to send the email.

Why are rewards sometimes unavailable?
Optional rewards are offered through video ads. When no ad is ready, the button shows that it is unavailable. Try again after a short while.
