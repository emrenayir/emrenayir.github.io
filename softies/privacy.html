Privacy Policy

Softies: Squishy Puzzle · Updated 30 October 2026

Softies is developed by Emre Nayir, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

Request deletion of your Softies account and data — available even if you have uninstalled the game.

Player accounts and online progress

You do not need to enter an email address or password to play. Online features use a player account provided by Unity Gaming Services. The game can create an anonymous player identifier automatically. On supported Apple devices, you may also connect through Game Center; Unity receives the Game Center player identifier and authentication information needed to link or recover your Softies account. Your Apple password is not shared with Softies.

The game sends completed-level identifiers to its backend, which stores completion records in Unity Cloud Save and calculates your leaderboard score. A generated player name, score and rank may be shown to other players on the leaderboard. These online records are linked to your Softies player identifier. They are not a complete backup of all items and settings on your device.

Softies does not ask for your real name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. If you contact support, I receive the email address and information you choose to send.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, boosters, unlocked items and settings are saved on your device. Completed levels are also sent to the online services described above, and gameplay events may be sent to analytics. Removing local app data does not delete your online account or the data already held by service providers.

Analytics

The game uses GameAnalytics to understand how levels are played so they can be balanced and improved. GameAnalytics receives:

  • a device or vendor identifier, and the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking;
  • device model, operating system version, app version, language and country;
  • gameplay events, such as levels started, completed or failed, time spent, boosters used and ads shown.

No name, email or other contact detail is sent. See the GameAnalytics privacy notice.

Advertising

Softies shows optional rewarded video ads and occasional ads between levels through Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners. To deliver ads and prevent fraud, these services may receive your IP address, device information, the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) and interactions with ads.

The game asks advertising partners not to personalize ads and opts out of the sale or sharing of personal data. On iOS, the system asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking; you can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See the Unity game player privacy policy.

To report an inappropriate ad, use Settings › Privacy › Report an ad or email me. Include the advertised app or product, approximately when the ad appeared, and a screenshot if available. The game opens an email draft; it does not send a report without your action.

Service providers

Unity Gaming Services processes account, cloud progress and leaderboard data to provide online game features. GameAnalytics processes gameplay and technical data to help improve the game. Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners process data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Their privacy notices explain their processing, retention and privacy request procedures. Apple handles your Apple and Game Center accounts under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to me.

Children

Softies is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me so I can investigate and arrange deletion where applicable.

Delete your Softies account and data

Request deletion by email

You can request deletion at any time by emailing emrenayir49@gmail.com with the subject Softies account and data deletion request. This option is available even if you have uninstalled the game or cannot access it. You do not need to reinstall the game to submit a request.

Say whether you played on iOS or Android and include any player identifier, leaderboard name or earlier support reference you already have. If you do not have these details, say so; I will reply if more information is needed to locate and verify the relevant records. Do not send passwords. Replies usually arrive within a few days, and I will explain the expected completion time and any data that must be retained when responding.

An account deletion request covers your Softies account, cloud progress, leaderboard entry and data associated with that account. You can also use this email to request deletion of analytics, advertising or support data. I will review the request and coordinate deletion with the appropriate service providers for records that can be located and verified. An email request cannot remotely erase data stored only on your device; you can remove local app data separately.

Delete from the game

If your version includes account deletion, open Settings › Privacy › Delete account and game data and confirm. An internet connection is required for online deletion. This removes your Softies account, cloud progress and leaderboard entry, and resets progress, coins and unlocked items on this device. Keep the game open until it confirms completion. If the game reports that deletion could not finish, reconnect and try again; a failed attempt is not a completed deletion. You can continue playing offline after deletion.

This does not delete your Apple or Game Center account, or automatically erase separate analytics and advertising records held by providers. You can use the email request above for those records, if your version does not have the deletion option, or if in-game deletion does not finish.

Retention

Online account and gameplay records are kept while the account remains active, until deleted through the game or a verified request. A minimal player-associated deletion status may remain if cleanup fails after account deletion; it is used only to prevent old sessions from restoring deleted game data and to finish cleanup. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Analytics and advertising records are subject to the providers' retention and deletion policies; deleting the app alone does not remove those records.

Your choices and rights

  • Limit tracking through your device's privacy settings.
  • Request deletion of your Softies account and game data by email or use the in-game option described above.
  • Ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data, or ask about withdrawing consent, by emailing me. Available rights depend on where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
